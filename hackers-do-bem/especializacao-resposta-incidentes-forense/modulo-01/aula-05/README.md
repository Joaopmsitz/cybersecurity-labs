## Overview

This lesson explores the infrastructure, technologies, processes, and resources required to establish a Computer Security Incident Response Team (CSIRT).

The activities analyze different infrastructure models, from a basic CSIRT environment to a virtualized architecture and an enterprise-scale network. They also examine an incident response workflow and discuss the services, tools, and operational practices required to manage security incidents.

The lesson emphasizes the importance of:

- Redundant connectivity and high availability.
- Network segmentation and laboratory isolation.
- Virtualization and centralized storage.
- Enterprise network monitoring and traffic analysis.
- Incident triage, analysis, and case management.
- Secure communication channels.
- Incident documentation and knowledge management.
- Business continuity and disaster recovery.
- Remote investigation capabilities.
