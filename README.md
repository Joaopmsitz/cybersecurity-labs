# Cybersecurity Labs

Hands-on cybersecurity portfolio documenting practical learning across SOC investigations, defensive security, cybersecurity fundamentals, and authorized training platforms.

This repository focuses on documenting the investigative process: what was examined, which evidence mattered, how findings were interpreted, and what was learned.

## About

I am a Systems Analysis and Development (ADS) student and IT professional building practical skills for cybersecurity roles, with a focus on Security Operations, alert triage, incident investigation, log analysis, and security infrastructure.

This repository documents guided training, practical exercises, and authorized lab environments. A tool or topic appearing here reflects documented study or lab practice, not a claim of production experience.

## Areas of Practice

- **SOC and incident investigation:** Reviewing alerts, collecting relevant evidence, evaluating indicators, and documenting conclusions.
- **Threat and artifact analysis:** Phishing, suspicious files and scripts, web-attack indicators, and Indicators of Compromise (IOCs), where documented.
- **Security operations:** SIEM concepts, detection engineering, threat hunting, and incident-response workflows.
- **Security fundamentals:** Hashing, cryptography, access control, hardening, and digital-forensics concepts.
- **Technical documentation:** Repeatable steps, evidence, observations, conclusions, and lessons learned.
