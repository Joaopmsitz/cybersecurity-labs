# João Paulo Moreira de Sousa | Cybersecurity Portfolio

Cybersecurity portfolio documenting hands-on learning across SOC investigations, Hack The Box, networking, digital forensics, and Microsoft Security.

This repository contains structured training, guided labs, and investigation exercises. The goal is to document the reasoning behind each activity: what was examined, which evidence mattered, how findings were interpreted, and what was learned.

## About

I am a Systems Analysis and Development (ADS) student and IT professional building practical skills for cybersecurity roles, with a focus on **Security Operations (SOC), alert triage, incident investigation, log and network analysis, and security infrastructure**.

This repository primarily documents platform-based training exercises and practical labs. A tool or topic appearing here reflects documented study or lab practice, not a claim of production experience.

## Areas of Practice

- **SOC and incident investigation:** Reviewing alerts, collecting relevant evidence, evaluating indicators, and documenting conclusions.
- **Threat and artifact analysis:** Phishing, suspicious files and scripts, web-attack indicators, and Indicators of Compromise (IOCs), where documented.
- **Networking:** Protocols, addressing, switching, routing, packet analysis, and troubleshooting fundamentals.
- **Security fundamentals:** Hashing, cryptography, access control, hardening, and digital-forensics concepts.
- **Technical documentation:** Repeatable steps, relevant evidence, observations, conclusions, and lessons learned.

Tools, methodologies, and findings are documented in their respective lab write-ups. The presence of a topic in this portfolio does not imply professional experience with every listed technology.
